SVP

Keep your eyes on the road.

Spatial UX/UI — 2024

Overview The Problem Design Direction Experience System Final

SVP puts guidance where the driver is already looking, so the road never leaves their sight.

The Problem

Navigation helps us drive. Looking at it can also take our eyes off the road.

What if navigation could stay in the driver’s line of sight?

Design Direction

Jonathan

36 · Uber / Lyft driver · San Francisco

“I need to know where I’m going without constantly checking another screen.”

  • Long hours driving
  • Night visibility
  • Hands-free

Pain points

Checks navigation frequently while driving.

Faint lane markings are difficult to read at night.

Complex intersections require quick decisions.

Goal

Clear, reliable guidance without looking away.

Motivation

Finish the night’s rides without second-guessing a turn.

Insight

Navigation needs to be understood at a glance, without pulling attention away from the road.

Experience

Set a destination without looking down.

Ask for help without taking a hand off the wheel.

Sharpens the lane when the lane is hard to see.
